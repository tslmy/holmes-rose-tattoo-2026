Phase 1 bake-off results

Rendered from docs/bakeoff-results.md, a working record of the project.

Iteration 8: Phase 2 closes: 65 of 80 redrawn, leftovers fixed (2026-11-21)

  • Room-object overrides never reached the game. The runtime reads every scene sprite from sprites/<resource>/, but since iteration 2 the pack recipe copied room objects (scene_NNN/room_NNN) next to the background, so every room object drew its 1996 frame over the redraw. In-game pixels in rooms 29 and 61 matched the 1996 frames exactly. tools/build_remaster_pack.py now builds the pack and puts room and cAnim overrides under sprites/. This alone fixed the room 1 wall cover, room 44's fan box and room 29's railing, bushes and bench (LMY-16, 17, 53).

  • Objects of other story states (not in the plate) are re-based on the redraw like animated ones (ambience_sprites).

  • Objects gate: objects the detector misses in the 1996 room itself are listed as unjudgeable and never count as missing, so the original passes the gate it is judged by.

  • Room 81 takes the original's red cast ([assemble] tint_rooms); its natural-colour card inset keeps the redraw's colours. Its sign reads an invented "MRS. MONTAGUE'S ARII CARD ROM" (LMY-44, sign handling).

  • Map (LMY-19): tools/redraw_rosetattoo_map.py redraws MAP.VGS in 4x3 overlapping tiles on the 9B, colour-matches each tile to its source and blends them (remaster/maptiles.py, delegated to OpenCode; its neighbour lookup was fixed after review). In game, icons, scrolling and travel work.

  • Rooms: QA re-run on the 11 rooms affected by the gate change (+2, 16, 21, 22, 39, 41), then two more repair seeds (L2-repair-s19, -s31) for the rooms still on the baseline (+2, 20). Room 2 failed by eye every time (the bearskin rug becomes a live animal; the bullet holes sit in a pasted patch) and is rejected. 65 of 80 redrawn. The last round added one room, so Phase 2 stops here.

  • Rooms that keep the ESRGAN baseline, with reasons:

    Rooms Reason

    92 the 1996 close-up is already photographic; faithful redraws change it too little (decision, LMY-54)

  • In game (pack generated/playable-overrides-2x-remaster-it8, rebuilt engine in work/engine/scummvm-playtest): all 80 scenes load; a dump of every room (work/playtest/it8-survey/) shows no old-art rectangles. Minor leftovers: a blurry rubble patch in room 6, a blurry smoke plume in room 74.

Iteration 7: objects-gate calibration (2026-11-21)

  • Calibration (qa.partial_match): a redraw detection that misses the IoU 0.3 match still finds the object when its centre lies inside the expected box grown by 25 %, within half a box length of the expected centre, at 0.15-2.5x its area. Of 924 unmatched detections across all QA records it accepts 189; a spot check of 12 found 11 right (the wrong one: room 44's "Strong Room Door" matched the curtain).
  • The original must pass the gate it is judged by (rule from the plan, 2026-11-21). The detector ran on the ESRGAN plate of each of the 20 baseline rooms as if it were a redraw (work/qa-calib/orig_objects.json):
    • passes before and after: 4, 5, 25, 39, 61, 71, 81, 91, 92;
    • passes only after calibration: 24, 29 (the old gate misjudged the original);
    • fails both: 2, 16, 20, 21, 22, 33, 36, 41, 54. The detector misses objects such as a lamp, a mail slot or a waste bottle in the 1996 room itself, so the objects gate cannot judge these rooms.
    • QA now computes this per room (qa.original_objects, cached in work/qa/scene_NNN/objects_original.json) and only honours partial matches where the original passes.
  • Result after re-running QA on the 20 rooms: 2 more rooms (29, 61): 59 of 80 redrawn.
    • Room 2 passed on a partial "Lamp" match, but its original fails the gate, so it stays on the baseline.
    • Room 92's only qualifying redraw (L2-clean-s41) passed QA but fails by eye: it invents a large lit brass lamp over the right margin and shows a pasted patch at the magnifier. It is rejected through the new [assemble] rejected list. The faithful seeds (s53, repair) pass QA with a change of 19.2, just under bakeoff_min_change (20), so the room keeps the baseline.
    • Rooms 29 (park) and 61 (drawing room) checked by eye against the 1996 art: composition, objects and exits kept (work/assemble/calib-it6-new-rooms.jpg).
  • Play-test engine bugs from iteration 6 (rooms 33/36 crash, room 57 hang, rooms 18/68 "To the City") turned out to be side effects of the play-test's post-load scene jump, not engine bugs (Linear LMY-24 to LMY-26; the hook fix is LMY-52, Phase 4).
  • In game (work/playtest/it7/ingame-29-61.jpg): room 61 is clean. Room 29 shows old-art layers over the redraw: a dark railing across the middle and the swaying foreground bushes (animated room objects still on the upscaled 1996 frames).
  • Pack generated/playable-overrides-2x-remaster-it7: all 80 scenes load.

Iteration 6: repair pass for near-miss rooms, UI play-test (2026-11-21)

  • The 20 rooms still on the baseline are near-misses: each one's best of eight seeds fails one QA gate (two for rooms 33 and 54): obstacles 8 (something added on the walkable floor), objects 8 (one small hotspot object not found), vlm 4 (people added), order 1, floor 1.
  • L2-repair variant (redraw_bg.repair_source / _run_repair, spec and acceptance test by Opus, implementation by OpenCode on qwen3.8-27b): takes the best near-miss, removes QA's blocking boxes and added people with one edit pasted back only around them, and restores missing objects by pasting the plate's pixels and harmonising them with a second edit. Only for objects the detector did not see in the redraw at all: in round 1 most "missing" objects were present but a little off position, and restoring them made duplicates (room 16's bottle got a second copy with an invented label). Enqueued for those 20 rooms only (L2-repair jobs alone, so the other levels' redraws are not re-planned).

The rest still fail mostly on the objects gate, which misses small objects that are present but displaced (rooms 2, 5, 16, 22, 41, 92): a QA calibration question, not a redraw one. Room 81 lost the 1996 scene's deep red tint (review item room81-red-tint).
  • UI play-test (session playtest-exits-ui, tools/playtest_ui_suite.py on branch claude/playtest-ui, findings in work/playtest/ui-findings.md): exits, map, inventory, journal and scrolling render cleanly on it5. Iteration 5: cutscene animations, play-test tooling (2026-11-21)

  • Cutscene animations (cAnims) had no overrides. The 767 cAnims (Holmes picking something up, a door opening, a fire) copy the 1996 room behind the figure. 498 are in redrawn rooms, and in 47 of the 55 redrawn rooms at least one would flash an old-art rectangle (up to ~200x200 px: rooms 93, 94, 74, 45). The per-room captures never showed this because nothing triggers a cAnim at room start (work/assemble/canim-audit.json).
  • Fix: assemble.canim_sprites re-bases every cAnim frame like an animated room object. Background pixels take the room's 2x background; figure pixels come from the October iteration-1 upscales ([assemble] canim_upscaled, enhanced/canimations-remaster-20261031, read-only); mirrored cAnims are stored unmirrored. Two cAnim-only rules: brightness-only matches stay old art (a swinging door is not a glow), and background must form regions at least 3 px wide (no speckle on moving coats). The pack serves 33,189 frames as sprites/scene_NNN/canim_NNN/frame_iii@2x.png, cloned from work/assemble (APFS clones, no extra disk). Pack: generated/playable-overrides-2x-remaster-it5; all 80 scenes load (smoke test).
  • cAnim figures are still the October upscales of the 1996 art; redrawing them is Phase 3/5. Where a cAnim moves part of the room (the 221B door in 01WOUT) that part is old art over the new room.
  • Written by Opus, not delegated: the model host <LAN host> went down mid-task (review item gpu-host-down-it5).
  • Play-test: computer use drives a local wrapper app (ShcrtPlaytest.app around the patched binary) in the background: screenshots and left clicks work, Holmes walks and looks. The verb menu (Open, Use) is a right click, which needs full-screen control, so cAnims and most exits cannot be triggered in the background. A scripted-input and frame-dump hook in the engine is proposed as its own task. Checked offline instead: frames composited over the room (work/assemble/it5-canims.jpg).
  • Verified in-game, headless: the engine hook from claude/sherlock-playtest-input (now applied in scummvm-src) with tools/playtest_rosetattoo.py opens the wardrobe in room 3 through the verb menu. During 03WARD the it4 pack shows the 1996 wardrobe rectangle and the it5 pack the redrawn wardrobe (work/playtest/wardrobe-it4-vs-it5.jpg). About 15 s per run, no window.
  • cAnim play-test suite (tools/playtest_canim_suite.py): survey each room's visible hotspots from the save, then for every hotspot whose verb plays a cAnim directly, right-click, pick the verb, dump the cAnim, on both packs. From validation/saves/rosetattoo.000, 53 triggers are visible; 24 play (the others are not yet offered by the story state: the verb is missing from the menu). In all 24, it4 shows old-art patches during the animation and it5 does not (work/playtest/canims-it5/sheet-*.jpg). Note: the room file's cAnim number in an object's uses is 1-based; the engine's and the override's are 0-based.
  • From the second save (validation/contour-slot1-playtest/rosetattoo.001, a later story state) 34 play, 14 of them new: 38 distinct cAnims verified in-game, every one clean on it5 and patched on it4 (work/playtest/canims-it5-slot1/new-cAnims.jpg).
  • Found by the play-test: static objects that only appear in other story states have no override, because room_sprites follows the plate's layers (one flag state). 535 such objects sit in 48 redrawn rooms; most are props (banners, a newspaper, pebbles), which are old art by design like the characters. The visible case is the wall cover 01WLLCVR in room 1, a 1996 patch of wall over the new window.
  • Found by the play-test: the ceiling fan in room 44 (44FAN, an animated room object since iteration 4) shows 1996 wallpaper between its blades in a box around the fan, because the redraw draws its own fan in another pose and has no wall behind it. Needs a redraw without the fan, or the fan's region filled.

Iteration 4: relative QA gates, ambience v2, extra seeds (2026-11-19)

  • Floor gate made relative. Calibration on iteration 3: the VLM floor check ("is every cyan walk zone on ground?") failed the untouched 1996 original in 18 of 23 rooms, because walk zones overlap furniture. A failed floor check now counts only when the same check passes on the original (cached per room in work/qa/scene_NNN/floor_original.json). The objects gate tolerates one confident miss when at least 85% of objects are found and the VLM diff does not report that object removed. Re-running QA on the existing redraws (no new images) took redrawn rooms from 22 to 49 of 80.
  • Checked by eye: the 27 newly passing rooms (1, 7, 11, 12, 14, 19, 26, 30, 32, 34, 37, 38, 40, 45, 52, 53, 56, 67, 80, 82, 83, 93-98) keep layout, furniture and floor; no missing or invented objects. Minor: a darker rectangle near the scissors in 93 and 94, and the paper props on the chemistry table change look.
  • Ambience v2 (delegated): scaled animated objects are cut at their drawn box, and small-delta pixels (water shimmer, scene 35) ride on the redraw as a delta. Finding: the scaled objects in redrawn rooms (the scene 60 doorman, figures) are real cut-out sprites (background fraction 6-16%), not old-room patches, so they correctly stay old art; the dark doorway in 60 is the door-opening animation.
  • Shared static images: a room image used by two layers at the same box (a sound trigger listed twice, e.g. 60SND) was skipped as ambiguous and showed as an old-art square; it is now cut once (11 objects in 8 rooms).
  • Extra seeds (L2-clean-s53/s11/s23/s29) are planned only for the 31 rooms with no passing redraw. The first run found the image server (<LAN host>:8000) down, and a run during the outage overwrote the original four seeds of those rooms with unavailable records, so all 8 seeds were redrawn (248 jobs, 9.5 h). 9 more rooms pass: 58 of 80 are redrawn (3, 15, 23, 42, 50 on s53; 18, 28 on s29; 27 on s11; 49 on s23). 22 rooms keep the ESRGAN baseline. Checked by eye: all 9 backgrounds are faithful; minor changes are the painted Laocoön now white marble (28), the blue curtains grey (50) and the window in 49 now a street.
  • In-game (pack playable-overrides-2x-remaster-it4, all 80 scenes load): 8 of the 9 new rooms look right. Room 18 did not: old-art streaks covered the new walls and steps. The cause is not its sprites (the capture was the same with every room override removed) but the engine's haze: in rooms 7, 8, 18, 53 and 68 the original fogs the screen through a palette lookup the hires compositor cannot reproduce, so it shows the native 1996 pixels wherever the haze applies (scummvm-src/engines/sherlock/README-haze-reference.md). assemble now keeps the ESRGAN baseline for these five rooms (HAZE_ROOMS), where those pixels blend in; 18 now looks coherent, and 7 and 53 (redrawn since iteration 2, with the same ghosting) look much the same either way, as the haze covers most of them. 55 of 80 rooms are redrawn. The morgue sheet in 15 is a 1996 cut-out sprite and shows its dither.

Approved by the user after iteration 2, which still showed 1996-art rectangles around animated room objects and many candidates rejected for added people.

Redraw variants L2-clean-s47/s19/s31/s41 set remove_people: people the redraw added (detected with no counterpart in the input) are painted out by a second edit of the redraw, pasted back only inside their feathered boxes. The cutoff is now 0.2 with up to two re-detected passes (people_left recorded). All 320 redraws re-ran.
  • Results: 141 of about 240 checked redraws had added people; cleanup cleared them in 112 (36 in one pass, the rest in two). QA passed 37 of 320 candidates; the VLM gate failed 100 (iteration 2: 218), floor 176, objects 132, obstacles 110, warp 15, text 10, order 8. 22 of 80 rooms now use a redraw (iteration 2: 14): 6, 9, 10, 13, 17, 31, 35, 43, 44, 46, 47, 48, 51, 55, 57, 58, 59, 60, 69, 73, 74, 77; the other 58 keep the ESRGAN baseline.

  • Left for iteration 4 (in-game): old-art patches remain where an animated object's frames differ from the plate (scene 35 pools, scene 74 shed lettering, scene 59 block left of the stove), a rectangle seam in scene 69's left window, low-res patches in scenes 6 (rubble) and 60 (window), and an odd ceiling shape in scene 43.

(b) Z-Image Turbo + ControlNet Union 2.1, depth + HED

  • Where it ran: on this Mac. The image server's API accepts only the three FLUX klein models and rejects extra fields, its source is on the other laptop, and SSH to it is closed.
    • The zimage-controlnet backend has allow_local = true. Every other local backend stays off.
    • The weights re-downloaded on the first job: about 23 GB with DepthPro. Free disk went from 60 to 37 GB.
    • Runs were one job at a time, 9 steps, --low-ram. Each took 250-290 s (755 s for the first, including the download). Peak memory was 22 GB.
  • The sweep: depth and HED, both at 0.65, 0.8 and 0.95; text-to-image from the visual brief. These variants use base level L1: an identity warp plus the layer-offset gate.
  • By eye:
    • The layout is pinned. 50/50 blends with the original show almost no doubled edges in any room at any strength, and no people are invented.
    • But the pictures are neither photoreal nor a sharper original. They are murkier: brown-grey, foggy, soft and low-detail.
      • Scene 36 dissolves into fog and its people become ghosts.
      • Scene 9 loses the bookcases and the green lamp, and its armchairs turn red.
      • Scene 1 loses "221B", the headline and the cobbles.
    • The VLM agrees: photoreal 2-4/10 (9B L2 scores about 9) and palette 2-5/10.
  • A measurement bug found and fixed.
    • The first QA pass failed every room on warp and offsets, with a p95 of hundreds of px, and found 0 of 8 objects in scene 1.
    • Cause: SIFT found nothing in common between the flat 1996 render and the retextured image. The resulting nonsense homography (scale 0.017) then drove object prediction and the warp fit.
    • geometry.homography_degenerate now detects that case. Object positions are then predicted with the identity, and the warp is fitted on the object anchors alone; thresholds are unchanged. With it, scene 1's p95 dropped from 587 to 8-19 px.
    • Rooms whose objects the detector can't find in the murky images (26, 36) still measure badly. Those fail on objects and vlm regardless.
  • Verdict: this configuration is not a route to photoreal rooms. It removes the invented-people problem only by replacing the picture with a worse one. Untried: canny-only or lower control, more steps, a tile/img2img hybrid, or a ControlNet on the server. Queued as bakeoff-1c-controlnet.

What the levels are

The image server takes strength (share of the input kept, mflux semantics), steps, guidance and up to 8 reference images.

  • L0: img2img at strength 0.6.
  • L1: img2img at strength 0.45.

All three use 8 steps. Geometry ships an identity warp for L0/L1, so their drift must stay under 2 and 4 px p95. For L2 it ships the fitted room warp.

The L2 room warp, honestly

The warp model is m(p) = s·(p + d(p)): a global scale times a smooth displacement on a 40 px grid. It is fitted from SIFT + MAGSAC, DIS optical flow and OWLv2 object anchors.

Where it works. When the model keeps the composition, the fitted warp is invertible and hotspots land on their objects. On scene 9 (9B) the hotspots sit on the paintings, busts, fireplace and seated man, and the walk zones sit on the floor. On scenes 4 and 1 they line up as well. Across all L2 results, 18 of 21 pass the warp gate (det > 0.05, stretch ≤ 1.6, round-trip ≤ 0.5 px), with a median p95 drift of 11 px for FLUX.2.

Where it fails.

  • Recomposition is beyond a smooth warp. 9B scene 36 (p95 75 px, stretch 1.66) and 4B scenes 18 and 9 failed. On 4B scene 9, matching found nothing usable, so the stretch is a sentinel.
  • Zoom or crop is only partly handled. Scene 26's zoom is absorbed by the scale term, but a walk zone ends up under furniture.
  • Added objects go unnoticed. The warp moves existing hotspots and has no idea that a new obstacle appeared, such as the side table and floor lamp 9B added in scene 9. The object gate checks only objects that exist in the original.

Not yet verified. Walk-zone overlays were checked by eye only on scenes 26 and 9. The engine-side warp (Phase 4) has not been built, so no hotspot has been clicked in-game.
