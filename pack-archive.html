Pack archive: what each pruned art pack was, and where its samples are

Rendered from docs/pack-archive.md, a working record of the project.

On 2026-11-22 free disk was ~8 GB, under the runner's 15 GB floor (min_free_disk_gb), blocking Phases 3 and 4 (Linear LMY-56). The repo held ~66 GB of generated packs (generated/, enhanced/, mods/). Most were superseded intermediate packs. Each one is summarised below. A few representative files were kept as APFS clones under work/pack-archive/<tree>__<pack>/ (gitignored; local only), so the article can still show what each method looked like and where it went wrong. work/pack-archive/index.json lists every sample and its path in the original pack.

The samples are mostly the same views in every pack, so packs can be compared side by side:

  • Backgrounds: golden scenes 4 (the sign on the police station, the writing test), 9 (the dark room), 26 (the billiard-academy interior with the engine fixtures) and 36 (a scrolling street) where the pack has them. The Claude iteration packs add rooms that changed between iterations (6, 29, 44, 57, 92).
  • Sprites: Holmes (svgawalk_vgs) and Watson (watson_vgs) walk frames 24-27 and the cast frame 88.
  • Scene 26 pilots: room objects room_001..003 and the exit cutscene canim_000 (26HEXIT).
  • Manifests, generation JSON and prompt files where they exist. These record the settings.

Sizes are du -sh on 2026-11-22. See pilot-lessons.md for the original index of these trees and bakeoff-results.md for the iteration results.

Claude iterations it1-it8 (November 2026)

All of them were built the same way: FLUX.2 klein 9B L2 redraws on the image server, the Phase 1c QA gates, assemble (warp back onto the 1996 layout, re-base room objects and cAnims), plus enhanced/sprites-temporal. Each iteration is described in bakeoff-results.md, and work/ still holds every redraw and QA record behind them. The pack is the only part that is dropped. Packs from it5 on are ~3.2 GB because they carry 33,189 re-based cAnim frames. Spot checks on 2026-11-22 found no identical files between iterations, so each pack's blocks are its own.

August 2026: ChatGPT/Codex diffusion packs

Made before November by the earlier ChatGPT/Codex effort, on A1111 (SDXL, a remote PC at <LAN host>), locally through mflux, and with a CUDA SDXL run on Windows. The shared lesson (pilot-lessons.md): once the strength is high enough to look different, every model drifts the geometry, moves or invents objects and garbles signage. Characters were the weakest part. Most of the scripts that made these packs were removed too; code-archive.md says what each did and which commit still has it.

Pack Size Method / settings Did well Characteristic issues Samples
generated/neural-redraws-cinematic-full 611M A1111 SDXL img2img + Canny ControlNet, profile photographic-cinematic, Juggernaut-XL v9, denoise 0.4, 32 steps, CFG 4.5, CN weight 1.0. All 80 scenes. Also stores a background_faithful@2x.png per scene. Strongest restyle: photographic light and texture. Geometry and signage drift, invented detail. Fed every v2-v6 playable pack. bg + faithful variant for 4, 9, 26, 36; prompts and metadata for 4, 26

generated/neural-redraws-faithful-full 705M A1111 SDXL, profile photographic-faithful, DreamShaper XL turbo, denoise 0.36, 16 steps, CN 0.92. Its manifest records 1 scene in the last run. Less drift. Barely visible change. bg 4, 9, 26, 36 + metadata

generated/flux-geometry-locked 134M FLUX.1-schnell img2img with a hard resource/edge geometry lock (flux_redraw_rosetattoo_backgrounds.py), protect feather 8, edge threshold 50; scene 4 at strength 0.85, 24 steps. October 2026: ESRGAN + contour pilots (mods)

Real-ESRGAN 2x on every asset, then a series of scene-26 pilot packs that settled alpha, palette and contour handling (sprite-remaster.md, sprite-contour-audit.md). Each pilot pack is the ESRGAN backgrounds plus one more layer of sprite handling. The trees they were assembled from stay in enhanced/.
