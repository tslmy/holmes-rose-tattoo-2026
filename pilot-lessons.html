Pilot lessons: index of the dated trees

Rendered from docs/pilot-lessons.md, a working record of the project.

Iteration 1 left ~25 GB of dated output trees under enhanced/, mods/ and generated/ (all ignored by git). They stay on disk as a record of what was tried; deleting any of them goes through the review queue (review/queue.md), never a script. This page says what each one is and what it showed, drawn from the docs and manifests named in each row. Where a row says (name only), no doc describes it and the purpose is inferred from its name and manifest; treat that as a lead, not a finding.

Sizes are from du -sh on 2026-11-14.

The two lines of work

  1. August 2026: diffusion redraws of backgrounds (A1111 SDXL/ControlNet, then FLUX.1-schnell, Z-Image Turbo, an optional GPT Image pass) plus "lifelike" character-sheet redraws. Lesson: every model drifted geometry, moved or invented objects and garbled signage once strength was high enough to look different; only very low strength (FLUX --steps 12 --strength 0.25, 3 real steps) kept text, and that barely changes the look. Character sheets could not stand in for per-frame animation. (reproducing.md history, tools/README.md calibration notes, z-image-setup.md, dev blog.)
  2. October 2026: non-diffusion SR + engine work. Real-ESRGAN 2x on every background, room sprite, cAnim and shared sprite, followed by a series of small pilots on scene 26 to settle alpha, palette and contour handling, ending in the assembled candidate mods/rosetattoo-contour-candidate-20261031. (sprite-remaster.md, sprite-contour-audit.md, contour-selection-plan.md, scene-start-smoke-test.md.)

Iteration 2's design answers both: diffusion is allowed to move things, but a per-room warp absorbs the movement and object/writing gates check it (docs/remaster-plan.md); de-dithering and plate-first redraw address the noise and lighting mismatch the August runs suffered from.

What carries into iteration 2

  • Diffusion must never be trusted to keep geometry or writing by low strength alone: hence object/writing gates and the warp.
  • Characters were the weakest part of every August attempt: hence casting sheets plus per-frame identity-conditioned edits, judged composited.
  • The ESRGAN contour candidate is the fallback for any asset that fails a gate; keep mods/rosetattoo-contour-candidate-20261031 until a better pack passes the smoke test.
  • Tiled inference is safe for large images (map) on this Mac's memory.
  • The 20261031 scene-26 fixtures (bartender, 26HEXIT, Watson walk) are why scene 26 and 026/000 are in the golden set.
